1. The Operations Room (Master Alarms & Atmospheric Controls)
This is your number one priority before you touch a data port.
Where to find it: It is always located on the upper floor (usually the second or third level) of the CMD building.
Visual layout: Look for a large command deck lined with glowing computer screens overlooking a glass window.
Key feature: You will find two interactive wall panels side-by-side here. One controls the Settlement Alarms (shut this off to prevent dropships from spawning), and the other controls the Atmospheric Environment (which you can use to vent the building's air if you need to clear out guards).
Security level required: Level 3. You must clone the profile of a high-ranking guard or the Settlement Command officer to open this door cleanly.
2. The Data Center / Server Room (Power Classified Data Ports)
This is where your target data drops are located.
Where to find it: Usually located on the ground floor or tucked in the back corridors of the upper floors.
Visual layout: Filled with tall, vertical server racks blinking with blue or orange lights.
Key feature: Look for the Data Ports protruding from the walls or sides of the server racks. They feature an interactive console screen. If the port is behind a locked maintenance hatch, pull out your Maverick Suit Arc Cutter to slice the panel open.
Security level required: Level 2 or 3.
3. The Security Office (Authorization & Lockers)
Where to find it: Almost always right near the main entrance lobby of the building or just off the primary ground floor corridor.
Visual layout: Features a security desk, weapon racks, and lockers.
Key feature: Contain lockers that spawn physical engineering assets, as well as a terminal to track guard patrol routes.

Yes, you can absolutely disable the alarms completely ghost-style without killing, frying, or even alerting a single NPC. In Elite Dangerous: Odyssey, a pure stealth run is actually the most efficient way to farm data because it prevents the settlement from going into lockdown.
Here is the step-by-step "Ghost" method to reach and disable the alarm panel cleanly:
Step 1: Get the Map and Find a Guard
Before entering the CMD building, track down the layout and a security profile:
Walk up to any exterior terminal outside the building and select the Operations room. This tracks the room on your HUD.
Look for a patrolling guard outside the building or through a ground-floor window.
Crouch, sneak up behind them, and use your Profile Analyser (ensure it is toggled to Clone mode, not scan mode).
Do not look at them directly if they turn around. Once the download finishes, you have their security clearance (you need Level 3 for the Operations room).
Step 2: Infiltrate the Building
Walk through the front or side doors using your cloned clearance. Keep your weapons holstered. As long as you don't jump on tables, steal items in plain sight, or trespass in restricted zones where your cloned level isn't allowed, NPCs will completely ignore you.
Follow your HUD waypoint up the stairs toward the Operations Room.
Step 3: Deal with the Room's Inhabitants (Without Violence)
When you reach the Operations room, there is usually one or two scientists or guards inside.
Check their line of sight: NPCs have a strict cone of vision. Look at the direction their bodies are facing.
Use the environment: The master alarm panel is almost always tucked on a wall or side console. If a scientist is sitting at a desk facing away from the panel, you can simply crouch-walk right past them.
The Crouch Walk: Moving while crouched completely silences your footsteps. As long as you stay out of their front visual cone, they will not notice you interacting with the wall panels behind them.
Step 4: Power Down the Alarms
Approach the Alarms console panel.
Interact with it and select Disable.
Crucial: The panel will take a few seconds to shut down. Do not move until the progress bar completes.
Once it says Alarms Offline, you are completely safe from reinforcements. Even if a guard catches you downloading the Power Classified Data later and shoots at you, no dropships will ever arrive, and the base defenses will not activate.
